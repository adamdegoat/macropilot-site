The root cause was DXY strength mechanically inflating the Cu/Au ratio while simultaneously pressuring dollar-denominated risk assets — the adversarial process correctly identified this vulnerability but the system lacked the weight to act on it. Going forward, DXY, credit spreads, and cross-asset momentum are upweighted as early-warning leads; copper_gold is sharply downweighted until the copper_futures_term_structure shadow signal can confirm whether Cu/Au moves reflect physical demand or financial positioning.

How MacroPilot works

MacroPilot watches the big-picture economy through 12 market signals, decides which "regime" the world is in, and adjusts a model portfolio to match. Here is exactly when it does each thing (all times Singapore / SGT):
🔄
Every 15 minutes
Scans the signals
Pulls fresh readings on all 12 signals (interest rates, the fear index, gold, oil, the dollar, credit, and more) and refreshes this page. No buying or selling happens here — it's just keeping the data current so you always see live numbers.
📅
Once a day · 6:00 AM
Decides the regime & rotates the portfolio
This is the main event. It re-reads every signal, makes a regime call in three steps (propose → challenge it → finalise), and if the outlook has shifted it rotates the portfolio — moving money between stocks, bonds, gold, energy, crypto and cash to fit the new picture. It moves gradually (at most ~20% in one day) so it never overreacts to a single noisy day.
🔁
Once a week · Sunday 7:00 AM
Learns from its track record
It grades how its past calls actually turned out and adjusts how much it trusts each signal — the signals that have been right get more say, the ones that have been wrong get less. This is the "self-improving" part.
💡 In plain terms: it checks the data every 15 minutes, makes its one real decision each morning at 6 AM, and tunes itself every Sunday. So the portfolio usually changes at most once a day — and often not at all if nothing meaningful has shifted.
